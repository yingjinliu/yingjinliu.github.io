I am a Ph.D. candidate in Economics at Seoul National University. My research interests are in applied microeconomics, with a focus on gender and family economics. My job market paper uses panel data from Taiwan to examine how parents' gender role attitude relates to their children's gender role attitude from adolescence into adulthood. I also work on gender wage gaps in South Korea and across countries.

**I am on the 2026–2027 academic job market.**
